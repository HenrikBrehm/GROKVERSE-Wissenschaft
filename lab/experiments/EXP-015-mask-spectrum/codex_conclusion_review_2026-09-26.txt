# Codex Adversarial Review

Verdict: needs-attention

No-ship for the causal/algorithmic wording. The evidence supports a split-mask-associated ranking of the crossing+25 W_E top-5 in eight p=113, 30%-split runs (matched rbar 0.277 versus foreign-mask mean 0.499). It does not establish that the split selects or determines the algorithm's frequencies, and the reported permutation p-value is not computed correctly for ties.

Findings:
- [high] Same-seed matching cannot identify the split as the selector (lab/LIVE.md:14)
  Each mask for seed s is matched to a baseline whose initialization also uses seed s. The training stack seeds both the split's local torch.Generator and the model's global RNG with that same integer. Permuting masks therefore breaks both the split match and the shared-seed relationship. Since initialization already predicts K, matched<foreign cannot distinguish a split effect from same-seed RNG linkage or split-init interaction. EXP-014/016 are still uncompleted, so the statement that the training split “determines” learned frequencies exceeds this observational design.
  Recommendation: Replace causal wording with “M0 is associated with and predicts the run's terminal W_E frequency ranking under this configuration.” Require an orthogonalized init×split or engineered-split intervention before saying the split selects or determines frequencies.
- [high] K is not independently established as the algorithmic frequency set (lab/experiments/EXP-015-mask-spectrum/README.md:100-103)
  Phase 2 defines K solely as the top five W_E power modes at a split-dependent stopping time, generalization crossing+25. A split-dependent but functionally unused embedding feature, or an effect of measuring seeds at different training steps, would produce the same result. The direct top-5 overlap is only 1.125/5; the stronger result is rank association. Older seeds make K plausible as a proxy—41/50 W_E top-5 entries overlap the downstream nanda key sets—but EXP-015 never tests M0 against that functionally closer target on either phase.
  Recommendation: Describe the endpoint explicitly as “W_E top-5 at crossing+25,” not the learned algorithm's frequencies. The cheapest discriminating check is to rerun matched-versus-foreign M0 on stored seeds 0–9 using the preregistered final nanda neuron-to-logit key sets as K; this requires no training and directly tests the W_E-artifact alternative.
- [medium] The permutation p-value excludes tied statistics (lab/experiments/EXP-015-mask-spectrum/mask_spectrum.py:90-92)
  rbar is discrete, but the lower-tail count uses `null <= observed - 1e-12`, excluding permutations tied with the observed statistic. Inclusive ties are required for the conventional exact randomization p-value; excluding them is anti-conservative. With only eight seeds there are just 8! = 40,320 assignments, so Monte Carlo is unnecessary. The reported 4.99975e-5 is also exactly the 20,000-draw zero-hit floor and should not be presented as a precise p-value. Eight seeds themselves do not invalidate the association test, but this implementation does not correctly handle their ties.
  Recommendation: Enumerate all 40,320 assignments, count statistics less than or equal to the observed value including numerical ties, and report that exact p-value. Keep the interpretation limited to association under exchangeability.
