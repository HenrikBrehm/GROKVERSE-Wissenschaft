# Codex Adversarial Review

Target: branch diff against HEAD~1
Verdict: needs-attention

No-ship: the common test set removes the direct complement-set artefact, but invalid runs can enter the primary analysis, D is not an equal-strength control after nonlinear sampling, and censoring can overstate acceleration.

Findings:
- [high] Short runs are silently promoted to 8000-step censored observations (lab/experiments/EXP-018-split-speed/analyze.py:26-59)
  `row` accepts a result using only status, seed, and split hashes, while `gen_step` converts every missing crossing to `CAP`. Because the run ID does not encode `--steps`, a completed 50-step/debug run placed under `raw` has the expected path, is accepted as though observed through step 8000, and also causes `freq_ban.py` to skip the intended run. The same bug makes strict-threshold results look censored at 8000 when `--stop-after-gen 25` actually stopped observation shortly after the 0.95 crossing.
  Recommendation: Validate the complete protocol (`exp_id`, condition, cap, cadence, configuration, and commit). If the primary crossing is absent, require `steps_completed == CAP`; otherwise record censoring at the actual last evaluated step. Include cap/test identity in run IDs and add fixtures for short and early-stopped runs.
- [high] Arm D does not isolate spectral concentration from modulation-distribution effects (lab/experiments/EXP-018-split-speed/make_splits.py:61-67)
  Only the pre-clipping standard deviation of the latent wave is matched. E averages 5 cosines while D averages 56 and rescales them, so their higher moments and tails differ systematically. The floor in `q_from_wave` and weighted sampling without replacement are nonlinear, meaning equal latent SD does not imply equal post-clipping weights or inclusion propensities. Exact residue profiles, anti-diagonal coverage, commuted-pair coverage, and fixed-test coverage are also not balanced—only coarse CVs are reported. Thus E-versus-D can reflect waveform shape or train-to-test difficulty rather than spectral concentration; sharing the raw test examples does not remove those confounds.
  Recommendation: Construct D from E so it preserves the exact post-clipping weight multiset per anti-diagonal while spreading its spectrum, use common sampling keys, and enforce or stratify on residue, anti-diagonal, reversed-pair, and test-coverage metrics. Regenerate and freeze the splits before running.
- [high] The censoring substitution is not conservative for the claimed estimand (lab/experiments/EXP-018-split-speed/analyze.py:64-77)
  Right-censored crossing times are replaced by 8000 and then treated as exact log-ratios in the sign-flip test. A censored E run may generalize arbitrarily later than 8000, but substitution truncates its negative D/E contrast; both-censored pairs are forced to zero despite an unidentified direction. Those attenuated values can leave a positive mean and small p-value even when censor-compatible true times reverse the conclusion. The test therefore supports, at most, a capped-time endpoint—not the stated uncensored acceleration claim.
  Recommendation: Either explicitly preregister and interpret `min(gen, 8000)` as a restricted endpoint, or retain censoring intervals and use a paired survival/interval-censored analysis or worst-case randomization test. Do not declare acceleration unless it holds across all censor-compatible values.
