  `model.W_E.mul_(s)` scales every number embedding and the `=` token embedding. In this transformer, `W_E[token]` is added to unscaled `W_pos` and directly drives Q/K/V, residual, and ReLU paths without LayerNorm. Therefore scaling changes attention logits, the final-position query, activation regimes, gradients, and AdamW trajectories. Own F/G references do not remove this confound. A shifted window can consequently arise without any amplitude-threshold mechanism, yet the analyzer labels the predicted pattern “H40 gestuetzt” and the README claims EXP-039/040 separate three explanations.
  Recommendation: Do not emit a causal H40 verdict from this intervention alone. At minimum, leave the `=` row and DC component fixed, manipulate only the number-token nonconstant spectrum, add equal-norm/shuffled-spectrum and input-scale controls, and require matched step-0 attention, logits, and gradient diagnostics. Otherwise describe the result only as an association with W_E scaling.

Nothing measures or establishes that assumption. Moreover, the planned power spectra contain only squared coefficient norms, so they cannot recover the data-driven vector displacement `coef(t)-coef(0)` or distinguish growth from rotation/cancellation against initialization. The numerical coincidence with 1.40 therefore does not support the preregistered scaling law.
