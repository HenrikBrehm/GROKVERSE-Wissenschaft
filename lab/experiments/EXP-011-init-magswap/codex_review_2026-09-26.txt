Verdict: needs-attention

No-ship: the swap mechanics appear internally consistent, but the preregistered analyses can label disruption as steering, compare outcome-dependent endpoints, and accept mismatched runs. The distribution-preservation and “directional share” claims are also stronger than the design supports.

Findings:
- [high] d can declare steering without any gain of S frequencies (lab/experiments/EXP-010-init-swap/analyze.py:49-56)
  T is selected as the baseline init top-5, so the motivating premise already makes the baseline contrast |K_base∩S|-|K_base∩T| negative. If the intervention merely destroys that association and K_swap contains neither S nor T, the swap contrast is zero and d is positive; likewise, a large T loss can outweigh an S loss. The verdict still calls this “steuerbar”; reporting gain_S and loss_T secondarily does not constrain the primary decision. This does not establish that final frequencies followed the coefficients moved to S.
  Recommendation: Make S gain and T loss separate co-primary requirements, or use direct post-swap S enrichment against a rank-selection-aware null. Do not label the current aggregate contrast as steering.
- [high] Valid-pair checks accept unmatched runs and unverified interventions (lab/experiments/EXP-010-init-swap/analyze.py:37-46)
  Validity checks only completion status, split_hash, and the initial power vector. A completed artifact with a different seed metadata, condition, configuration, max_steps, stop-after-generalization value, library/platform, or training code can pass; stop-after-generalization is not encoded in the run directory. The check also ignores power_after and does not prove that the recorded swap occurred. EXP-011 separately accepts full and magnitude runs without confirming identical pair lists or post-swap spectra, so its direction contrast can subtract different interventions.
  Recommendation: Fail closed on seed, condition, m, all relevant config fields, max_steps, stop_after_generalization, versions/provenance, expected pair construction, and expected power_after. Store and compare a hash of the complete pre-intervention model state, and require EXP-010/011 pair lists and post-swap spectra to match exactly.
- [high] K_end is measured at a treatment-dependent stopping time (lab/experiments/EXP-001-freq-ban/freq_ban.py:309-320)
  Each arm stops at its own generalization crossing plus 25 steps, or at 15,000 if it never crosses. Because the intervention can change or prevent crossing, paired K_end values may come from widely different training steps; capped non-generalizers are still marked completed and included. Any time-dependent decay or consolidation of init-frequency membership can therefore enter d as an apparent swap effect.
  Recommendation: Use a fixed, common training-step endpoint for the primary comparison. Treat crossing-aligned measurements as secondary, require both arms to cross for them, and report trajectory/common-landmark sensitivity analyses.
- [high] The claimed exact sign-flip test lacks a valid sign-randomization null (lab/experiments/EXP-010-init-swap/analyze.py:62-65)
  The test flips observed d values as though their signs were exchangeable. There is no randomized assignment of baseline versus intervention labels, and the stated null concerns the mean rather than symmetry of the full d distribution. Because d is bounded, discrete, and constructed from asymmetrically selected top-versus-weak sets, symmetry is not guaranteed. The resulting p-value is therefore not an exact test of the preregistered null and can be anti-conservative.
  Recommendation: Base inference on an actual randomized component of the design, such as replicated randomized S/matching assignments with an explicit randomization null, or preregister a justified sampling model and confidence interval. Do not describe the current sign-flip p-value as exact.
- [medium] The ordered rank swap is not iid-distribution preserving (lab/experiments/EXP-001-freq-ban/freq_ban.py:108-122)
  The code orders T by descending power, sorts S by ascending frequency number, then zips them. Consequently, within selected S, the lowest k deterministically receives the largest top-order statistic, the next-lowest receives the second largest, and so on. Under iid-normal initialization those assignments would not be deterministic. Norm/direction independence only preserves normalized directions; it does not make this rank- and label-dependent norm reassignment jointly iid. Both swap conditions therefore introduce a systematic frequency-index/power relationship despite the README’s distribution-preservation claim.
  Recommendation: Randomize the T-to-S matching independently and establish exchangeability of the resulting transformation, or narrow the claim to preservation of the norm multiset/Frobenius norm and treat the intervention as rank-conditioned rather than iid-distribution preserving.
- [medium] d_swap minus d_mag is not an additive directional share (lab/experiments/EXP-011-init-magswap/analyze.py:42-45)
  The subtraction is a conditional contrast between full and norm-only interventions at the swapped norm spectrum. In nonlinear training it can include norm-direction interactions and cannot be interpreted as a share of the total effect without a direction-only arm. A near-zero mean can also result from opposing per-seed effects, and a nonsignificant two-sided test does not establish the README’s claimed approximate equality or magnitude-only explanation.
  Recommendation: Call this an incremental direction contrast, report its interval and heterogeneity, and preregister an equivalence margin if near-equality is the claim. Add a direction-only arm to form a 2×2 norm/direction design if main effects and interactions are to be separated.
